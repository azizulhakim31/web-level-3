OUR STORY

A little passion can grow into a forest.

Blooming Oasis began with a simple idea: plants should make everyday life feel more connected, calm and alive.

We started as friends swapping cuttings, learning from the occasional yellow leaf, and helping each other find the right plant for each room. That small shared hobby grew into a mission to make indoor gardening feel welcoming for everyone—from curious beginners to lifelong plant lovers.
