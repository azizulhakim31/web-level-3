    KNOW YOUR MONSTERA

    Care at a glance

    ☀️

    Light

    Bright, indirect light is ideal. Avoid harsh midday sun.

    💧

    Water

    Water when the top 2-5 cm of soil feels dry; drain excess water.

    🐾

    Pet safety

    Not pet-safe. Monstera can irritate pets if chewed. Keep out of reach.

    🪴

    Repotting

    Usually every 1-2 years, or when roots fill the pot. Use a draining mix.
