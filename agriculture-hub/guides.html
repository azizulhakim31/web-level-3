Farming Guides

Simple steps for healthier crops and better harvests.

1. Soil Preparation

Prepare the land before planting.

  1. Remove weeds and unwanted materials.
  2. Test soil pH and nutrient levels.
  3. Improve soil with suitable organic matter.
  4. Level the land and prepare planting beds.

Tip: Use soil-test results to choose amendments.

2. Seed Planting

Give seeds the right start for healthy growth.

  1. Choose quality seeds suited to your region.
  2. Plant at the recommended depth and spacing.
  3. Cover seeds gently with soil.
  4. Water carefully and monitor germination.

Tip: Follow crop-specific planting advice.

3. Irrigation Management

Provide crops with the right amount of water.

  1. Check soil moisture regularly.
  2. Water near the roots when needed.
  3. Prefer early morning irrigation.
  4. Avoid waterlogging and excessive watering.

Tip: Adjust watering to weather and crop needs.

4. Harvesting and Storage

Protect crop quality after maturity.

  1. Identify the correct maturity stage.
  2. Harvest using clean tools.
  3. Sort damaged produce separately.
  4. Store crops under suitable conditions.

Tip: Keep stored produce clean and dry when appropriate.
