Smart Pest Management

Protect crops through monitoring and responsible pest control.

What Is Integrated Pest Management?

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) combines monitoring, prevention, biological methods, and carefully selected treatments to reduce crop damage.

1. Prevention

  • Use healthy seeds and suitable crop varieties.
  • Remove weeds and infected plant residues safely.
  • Rotate crops when appropriate.
  • Maintain good field hygiene and plant spacing.

2. Early Detection

  • Inspect leaves, stems, and fruits regularly.
  • Look for holes, spots, eggs, and unusual wilting.
  • Use suitable traps to monitor specific pests.
  • Record pest numbers and crop damage.

3. Biological Control

  • Encourage beneficial insects where appropriate.
  • Protect natural predators and pollinators.
  • Use approved biological products when suitable.
  • Avoid unnecessary harm to helpful organisms.

4. Organic and Chemical Options

  • Consider locally approved, low-risk options.
  • Ask experts before applying homemade remedies.
  • Use registered pesticides only when necessary.
  • Follow label directions and protective measures.
