The archive

History deserves to be remembered.

The Liberation War of 1971 shaped the identity and future of Bangladesh. This learning archive brings historical material together to support remembrance, education and further research. Catalogue descriptions provide an introduction to the subject; verify each source before using it as a historical reference.

Keep the record alive

Every memory can teach us something.

Families, researchers and communities may hold letters, photographs or recordings that add context to history. Share material responsibly, include its source where possible, and ensure you have permission to submit it.
